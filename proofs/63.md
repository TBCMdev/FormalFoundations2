# $[x \not\equiv y ~ \equiv ~ y \not\equiv x]$

We observe for any $x, y$:

- Starting with the lhs, we get rid of the not equivalence **asap**, swap the equivalence in the bracketsand convert it back into a not equivalence:
    ```math
    x \not\equiv y 
    \\ \rightarrow \\
    \neg(x \equiv y)
    \\ \rightarrow \\
    \neg(y \equiv x)
    \\ \rightarrow \\
    y \not\equiv x
    ```
